Svetlana Antonovska

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Svetlana Antonovska (Светлана Антоновска, April 6, 1952 – October 7, 2016) was a Macedonian statistician who became known as the founder of contemporary statistics in the Republic of Macedonia. She headed the State Statistical Office of the republic from its founding in 1991 until 2001, brought the office into communication with several major international statistical organizations, and founded the first census of the republic.[1][2]
